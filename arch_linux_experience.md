# My Experience with Arch Linux and I Love it
- I've always been a big fan of linux and open source software. It not just allows you to have free-stuff but also have control over it. In this sense you can see how thing actually works in real-life and you can also have control over it. Even though I do understand why prefer do prefer to buy software, I still find myself very confused about the fact of so many people still buying tools like office 365, where now a days there are countless options that are open-source and easy to use. Even though some of this tools are harder to use than the paid ones, they offer a great long-term returns. Like if tools like latex exists why do people will still prefer to use Microsoft word for writing papers.
- Something which I can find myself doing a lot is finding tools that will not only make me more productive but as well extremely efficient with my workflow. This made me realize that software like Notion, ChatGPT and others are just systems design to sell you things rather than to boost your workflows. Like notion if you're not careful enough you might hit a limit with the number of blogs you're writing, or even worst you must paid to download your data. This made me conclude that I needed a chnage on perspective and I needed fast. After looking countless alternatives I end up realizing that the best option was to switch to linux and try a minimal setup.
